## Transportation Guide

### From Taoyuan International Airport (TPE)

- **Taxi or Uber:** Approximately 40–50 minutes.
- **Public transportation:** Take the Airport MRT to **HSR Taoyuan Station**, then take the Taiwan High-Speed Rail to **HSR Hsinchu Station**, followed by a taxi or Uber to National Tsing Hua University (NTHU).

### From Hsinchu HSR Station

- **Taxi or Uber:** Approximately 15–20 minutes.
- **Bus:** Several routes provide access to or near the NTHU campus:
  - **Route 182 / Shuttle Bus No. 5:** Direct access to NTHU.
  - **Routes 5608 / 5615:** Take a bus toward downtown Hsinchu and get off near NTHU.
  - **Blue Line city shuttle:** Transfer to a connecting route serving NTHU.

> **Recommendation:** For international participants or first-time visitors, taking a taxi or Uber is the easiest and most convenient option.

### From Hsinchu TRA Station

- **Taxi or Uber:** Approximately 10–15 minutes.
- **Bus:** Multiple routes serve the NTHU area:
  - **Bus No. 1（市區公車 1 路）:** Direct route to NTHU. Get off at the NTHU bus stop.
  - **Bus No. 2（市區公車 2 路）:** Stops near the NTHU campus.
  - **Green Line shuttle（綠線接駁車）:** A convenient city shuttle option.
