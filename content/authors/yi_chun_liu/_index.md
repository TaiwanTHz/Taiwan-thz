My personal website can be found at [Jenny Yi-Chun Liu's NTHU webpage](https://www.ee.nthu.edu.tw/jennyliu/wiki/index.php?title=Main_Page).

Dr. Jenny Yi-Chun Liu is an Associate Professor at National Tsing Hua University. She received her B.S. degree in Electronics Engineering from National Chiao Tung University in 2005, and her M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Electrical Engineering from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), in 2008 and 2011, respectively. Her doctoral research focused on self-healing millimeter-wave circuits and systems. She subsequently worked as a postdoctoral scholar at UCLA from 2011 to 2012 before joining National Tsing Hua University in 2012.

Dr. Liu's research focuses on millimeter-wave and sub-millimeter-wave devices, circuits and systems, with particular interests in THz communications and imaging. Her research also covers ultra-low-power wireless circuits and systems for biomedical applications and reconfigurable radio technologies.
